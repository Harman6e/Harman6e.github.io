Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Information shown publicly

Public listings can include a title, game, platform, region, price, description, key account details, warranty information, and screenshots. Sensitive login information must never be published.

Admin information

The admin dashboard uses Supabase authentication. Login sessions are handled by Supabase. The website must use only the browser-safe publishable or anon key. Secret and service-role keys must never be placed in website files.

Contact enquiries

When you contact Torn Deals through WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, or email, that service processes your message under its own privacy policy.

Storage

Listing records and public listing photos are stored in the connected Supabase project. Public listing photos can be viewed by anyone with their URL.

Data minimisation

Do not send passwords, recovery codes, backup codes, payment-card details, private account identifiers, or other credentials through public forms or listing photos.

Contact

Use the marketplace contact options to request correction or removal of listing information.
