Privacy

The order form stores the buyer's name, chosen contact information, message, selected product, order status, and submission time. Torn Deals uses this information only to respond to and manage the order.

Do not submit passwords, recovery codes, government identification, card information, or other sensitive credentials.

Product photos and public descriptions are visible to website visitors. Administrative data and customer order requests are restricted to the owner account.

To request correction or deletion of an order enquiry, contact Torn Deals through the support details on the homepage.
